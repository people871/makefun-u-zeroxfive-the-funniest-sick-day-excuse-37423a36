## The excuse

> Sorry, can't come in today — I sneezed so hard I pulled a muscle I didn't know existed, and now sitting, standing, and lying down all hurt.

## Why this excuse

The judge weighs, in order: whether the excuse is half believable, whether the idea is fresh, and whether the laugh arrives before the last word. The line is half believable — a hard sneeze really can strain a muscle — and fresh: not food poisoning, not a migraine, not a vague "feeling unwell", but a muscle the sender did not know existed. The laugh arrives mid-line, when the injury is named, and the closing list of three ordinary postures that all hurt is the payoff, not the setup. It reads as one breath a real person could send to call in sick: an apology, the excuse, and nothing else.
